# 🌿 SilverMove

**SilverMove** is a beginner-friendly web project designed to make exercise and gentle yoga information easier to approach for older adults.

Instead of focusing on advanced poses or high-intensity fitness, the project organizes movement around mobility, muscle strengthening, balance, gentle aerobic activity, accessible yoga and safety.

## 🎯 Why I Built This

A lot of fitness content online is presented around younger or highly active audiences. I wanted to explore a different approach: a simple, calm and accessible resource that gives older adults and their families an easy starting point for movement.

This project is also an exercise in turning a real-world problem into a small web application.

## ✨ Features

- Simple sample weekly plans
- Exercise library with filters
- Gentle yoga section
- Large, readable interface
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Safety guidance
- Evidence/source section
- No frameworks or external libraries required

## 🛠️ Built With

## 🧓 Activity Guidance

Public guidance from the CDC and WHO recommends that adults aged 65+ include a mix of aerobic activity, muscle-strengthening activity and balance-focused activity. The CDC currently describes at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week, muscle-strengthening activity on at least 2 days per week, and balance activities. WHO similarly recommends aerobic and strengthening activity, with additional balance-focused activity for older adults with poor mobility.

This project **does not prescribe those targets to an individual**. The sample plans are intentionally modest educational examples and should be adapted to a person's abilities and conditions.

## ⚠️ Safety

SilverMove is an educational web project, not a medical, physiotherapy or rehabilitation program.

People with health conditions, significant mobility limitations, recent surgery, a history of falls, or uncertainty about which movements are appropriate should seek appropriate professional guidance before starting or changing an exercise routine.

Use stable support for balance exercises when needed. Stop an activity if it causes pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or feels unsafe.
