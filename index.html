WHY SILVERMOVE?

Exercise should not feel intimidating.

Many fitness resources focus on high-intensity workouts or advanced poses. SilverMove focuses on practical movement that can fit into everyday life.

SAFETY FIRST

A good routine starts with listening to your body.

This project is an educational resource, not medical advice. People with health conditions, significant mobility limitations, recent surgery, or concerns about starting exercise should ask a qualified healthcare professional what activities are appropriate.

01Start gradually

Begin with a comfortable amount and build up over time.

02Use support

Keep a sturdy chair, wall or another stable support nearby for balance work.

03Don't push through pain

Stop a movement that causes pain, dizziness or feels unsafe.

04Make the space safe

Wear stable footwear and remove obvious trip hazards around the exercise area.
